      How scores are derived

      • Seniority (0–3) + Domain fit (0.5–3) + Location (0–2) + Differentiators (0–2).
      • Domain rewards IT leadership, enterprise apps, SaaS/cloud, AI, collaboration, vendor/governance, service management, data & platform. Penalizes security-only, ERP-config, and help-desk roles.
      • "Software/Engineering" titles are capped (dev-lean) and listed separately for review.
      • Two passes: every role gets a title-score (title + company + location); the top matches also get a deep-score re-scored against the full job description (requirement-theme coverage, penalized for hands-on-dev / help-desk / below-seniority signals).
      • Excluded per instruction: "Software Development Manager", "Development Manager", and hands-on dev-team leadership. US-only "remote" postings were dropped.
