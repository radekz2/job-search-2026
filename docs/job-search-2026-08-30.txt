HOW SCORES ARE DERIVED
==============================================================================
Score = Seniority (0-3) + Domain fit (0.5-3) + Location (0-2) + Differentiators (0-2).
  Seniority: Director/VP/Head/CTO = 3.0; Manager = 2.5; -0.5 if a narrow/specialist function.
  Domain: rewards IT leadership, enterprise apps, SaaS/cloud, AI, collaboration, vendor/governance,
          service mgmt, data & platform. Penalizes security-only, ERP-config, and help-desk roles.
  'Software/Engineering' titles are capped (dev-lean) and listed separately for review.
  Location: Toronto 2.0 > GTA 1.6 > Canada-remote 1.5 > other Ontario 1.3 > other Canada 1.0.
  Differentiators: AI/automation/transformation + enterprise/portfolio/global/platform scope.

TWO-PASS SCORING
  Pass 1 (title-score): every role is ranked from title + company + location metadata.
  Pass 2 (deep-score): the top matches are re-scored against their actual job-description
  text (requirement-theme coverage, plus penalties for hands-on-dev, help-desk, and
  below-seniority signals). Results are in the DEEP-DIVE section.

NOTES
- Pass-1 scores use title/company/location only; Pass-2 'deep' scores use the full job
  description. Both reflect fit against your aggregate resume profile (all 4 resumes).
- Deep-dive covers the top matches only; the remaining roles still carry title-based scores.
- Excluded per instruction: 'Software Development Manager', 'Development Manager', and hands-on
  dev-team leadership (Team Lead / Tech Lead). US-only 'remote' postings were also dropped.
- Sections 3 & 4 are 'Software Manager'-type roles; even high-scoring ones usually manage dev
  teams, so confirm each posting's actual scope before applying.
